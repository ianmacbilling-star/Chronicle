Hand off Story Master

Make this player the Story Master?

They take over running the campaign. You become a regular player and keep your access. Only the current Story Master can do this, and it takes effect immediately.

Remove Member

Remove this player from the campaign?

Their character will be released and become available to re-invite. Their tokens and account stay intact. They lose access to this campaign immediately.

Revoke Invite

Revoke this invitation?

The link will stop working. If the invitee already clicked it, they'll see an "invitation no longer valid" message. You can always create a new invite later.

Mark Session Ready

Marking this session Ready publishes it to your players.

Once any session in the campaign is Ready, players can no longer edit their own canonical characters — they'll have to fork the session to make changes. (Forking support is coming in a future update.)

You can switch back to Draft at any time. No data is lost when you do.
