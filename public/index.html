THE LORE — FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What transcript format does Campaignia need?
Anything goes — session notes, Discord logs, Roll20 exports, voice-to-text recordings, or your own summary. The more detail, the richer the output.
How does the AI know which moments matter?
Campaignia identifies combat, revelations, and emotional beats automatically. You can also give it director's notes — tell it exactly which scenes you want included. Click here to see some of the moments other users have made.
Can I use Campaignia for any TTRPG system?
Absolutely. D&D, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu, Blades in the Dark, Mothership — any system with a story worth telling. Campaignia works from the transcript, not the ruleset.
Do I need my own AI API keys?
No. Campaignia handles all AI costs on our end. Your subscription covers everything — no setup required beyond creating your account.
What does the exported PDF look like?
A print-ready graphic novel with cover page, AI-illustrated panels, narrative prose, and your character cast. Four layout styles, preview before you print. Click here to see our Library.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, always. Cancel anytime and keep access through your billing period. Your campaigns and sessions are always accessible — your stories don't disappear.
