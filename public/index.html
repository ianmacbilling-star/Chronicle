📜 THE LORE
FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What kind of transcript does Chronicle need?
Anything goes — session notes, chat logs from Discord or Roll20, voice-to-text recordings, or just your own summary written after the session. The more detail, the richer the output.
How does the AI know which moments matter?
Chronicle reads your full transcript and identifies combat encounters, dramatic revelations, character moments, and emotional beats. You can also give it director's notes before generating — tell it exactly what scenes you want included.
Can I use Chronicle for any TTRPG system?
Absolutely. D&D, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu, Blades in the Dark, Mothership — any system with a story worth telling. Chronicle works from the transcript, not the ruleset.
What does the exported PDF look like?
A print-ready graphic novel with a cover page, your session panels with AI-generated illustrations, narrative prose between panels, and your character cast. Choose from four layout styles and preview before you print.
Do I need to provide my own AI API keys?
No. Chronicle handles all the AI costs on our end. Your subscription covers everything — Claude for narrative intelligence, Flux for image generation. No setup required beyond creating your account.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, always. Cancel anytime and keep access through your current billing period. Your campaigns and sessions are always accessible even if you downgrade — your stories don't disappear.
