Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Last updated June 22, 2026
Operated by So It Begins, LLC (“Campaignia,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).
This Privacy Policy explains what information Campaignia collects, how we use and share it, and the choices you have. It works alongside our Terms of Service. By using Campaignia, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who We Are & Scope

Campaignia is a service operated by So It Begins, LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company. Campaignia turns your tabletop role-playing game sessions into illustrated graphic novels and related content. This policy applies to the Campaignia website and application and to the information we handle when you use them. It does not apply to third-party websites or services we link to or integrate with, which have their own privacy practices.

2. Information We Collect

Information you give us.

  • Account information — your name, email address, password (stored only in hashed form), and your date of birth or age confirmation, which we use to enforce our minimum-age requirement.
  • Your content — the session transcripts, notes, character and asset names and descriptions, uploaded reference images, and the narratives, images, storyboards, and books that the service generates from your input.
  • Order and shipping information — when you order a printed book, the recipient name, shipping address, and phone number you provide for fulfillment.
  • Payment information — your payment is processed by our payment provider, Stripe. You enter your card details directly with Stripe; we do not receive or store your full card number. We receive limited billing information such as the card brand, the last four digits, your subscription status, and whether a charge succeeded.
  • Communications — messages you send us (for example, support requests) and information you provide in them.

Information we collect automatically.

  • Technical and usage data — basic information such as your IP address, browser and device type, pages and features used, and timestamps, which we use to operate, secure, and improve the service.
  • Cookies and local storage — we use a session cookie to keep you signed in and a small amount of browser storage to run core features. See Section 7.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the service — create and manage your account, process your transcripts, generate narratives and images, build and render your books, and store your work.
  • To process payments, subscriptions, token purchases, and print orders.
  • To send you service-related email, such as account verification, password resets, and important notices.
  • To secure the service, prevent abuse and fraud, enforce our Terms, and comply with the law.
  • To maintain, troubleshoot, analyze, and improve Campaignia.

4. AI Processing of Your Content

Campaignia uses third-party artificial-intelligence providers to create your Output. When you generate content, the relevant input — such as your transcript text, prompts, and uploaded reference images — is sent to those providers so they can return the narrative or image you requested. We use Anthropic’s Claude API for text (transcript analysis, narrative writing, and in-app help) and fal.ai for image generation. These providers process your input on our behalf to perform the requested generation and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices. AI output can be unexpected or inaccurate; please review your Output before using or publishing it.

5. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We share information only as described below.

Legal and safety. We may disclose information if we believe in good faith it is necessary to comply with the law or a valid legal request, to enforce our Terms, to detect or prevent fraud, security, or abuse issues, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Campaignia, our users, or the public.

Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

6. The Public Library

Publishing to the Library is optional and entirely your choice. A published story is shown publicly under your chosen pen name (or, if you set none, without a name), can be read by anyone for free, and may be indexed by search engines. You can remove a story from the Library at any time; removal stops public display but does not delete your private copy, and copies that others already viewed or that remain in search-engine caches may persist outside our control.

7. Cookies & Tracking

We use a session cookie so the service can recognize you while you are signed in, and a small amount of browser storage to support core features. These are necessary to operate Campaignia. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party ad trackers. On our public pages — the landing page, the public Library, and shared story pages — we use Google Analytics to understand aggregate visitor traffic, which sets analytics cookies provided by Google; we do not run analytics inside your signed-in account. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings, but doing so may prevent you from signing in or using parts of the service.

8. Data Retention

We keep your account information and content for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. When you delete content or your account, we delete or anonymize the associated personal information and private content within a reasonable period, except for: content you published publicly; information retained in routine backups for a limited time before it is overwritten; and records we are required to keep to comply with the law, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements. Because storage egress is inexpensive, generated images may be retained while associated with your account and are removed when the related campaign, asset, or account is deleted.

9. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for notifying us of any suspected unauthorized use of your account.

10. Your Choices & Rights

You can review and update your account information from within the app, change your password, and manage or cancel your subscription. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by emailing support@campaignia.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) or the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) — including the right to know what we collect, to request deletion or correction, to opt out of any “sale” or “sharing” (which we do not do), and to not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We will verify your request through your account email before acting on it and will respond within the time required by applicable law.

11. Children’s Privacy

Campaignia is not directed to children under 13, and you must be at least 13 to create an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete the account and associated data. If you are under the age of majority where you live, you may use Campaignia only with the involvement and consent of a parent or legal guardian.

12. International Users

Campaignia is operated from the United States, and the providers we use may process and store information in the United States and other countries. If you access the service from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your location.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the version number and the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice. Your continued use of Campaignia after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

14. Contact

Questions or requests about your privacy? Contact So It Begins, LLC at support@campaignia.com.
