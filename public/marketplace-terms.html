Marketplace Terms

Version 1.0 · Last updated October 27, 2026
Operated by So It Begins, LLC (“Campaignia,” “we,” “us,” or “our”).
These Marketplace Terms apply when you list yourself on the Campaignia Marketplace or use Make contact to reach a member. They add to our Terms of Service, which still apply in full.

1. The Marketplace

The Marketplace is a directory where members can list themselves as artists, illustrators, writers, editors or Campaignia Specialists (people who build a whole book in Campaignia for someone), and where other members can contact them about work.

2. Listings Are Public

Anyone can see a listing, signed in or not. Your name, headline, description, links, rates and sample pictures are shown as you enter them. Your email address is never shown. Only list work you made yourself or have the right to show.

3. We Review Listings

A new listing appears only after we approve it. Changes to your approved listing are subject to review as well. We may decline, hide or remove any listing at any time, including one whose pictures appear to be someone else’s work.

4. Contacting a Member

Make contact sends your message by email to the member listed, with your account email address as the reply address, so the two of you can talk directly. Use it only to ask about work: no spam, no selling other services, and no harassment. We limit how many messages can be sent.

5. Arrangements Are Between You

Campaignia is not a party to any arrangement you make with another member. That includes price, payment, deadlines, quality, refunds, disputes, and who owns the finished work. We do not employ, endorse or vouch for anyone listed, and we take no fee. Agree the terms in writing, including whether the work may be used in your Campaignia book. Before you upload work someone made for you, make sure you have the right to use it (Terms of Service, section 4). Sections 11 (Disclaimers) and 12 (Limitation of Liability) of the Terms of Service apply to the Marketplace.

6. Your Information on the Marketplace

If you list yourself on the Marketplace, your listing is public: your name, headline, description, links, rates and sample pictures. If you use Make contact, we email your message, your name and pen name to the member you are contacting, with your account email address as the reply address. We keep a record of each message to stop abuse. Sample pictures are checked for watermarks in the same way as other uploads. Our Privacy Policy covers everything else.

7. Changes to These Terms

We may update these Marketplace Terms from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the version number and the “Last updated” date, and ask you to accept the new version the next time you update your listing.

8. Contact

Questions about the Marketplace? Contact So It Begins, LLC at support@campaignia.com.
