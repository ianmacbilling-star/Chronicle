You still talk about that day…

“The mishap.” “The find.” “The win.” “The first.” There are moments in our life that change everything. They deserve to be remembered and shared. And “you had to be there” is so unsatisfying.

Campaignia doesn’t reinvent your experience. It just gets it written down. It takes your notes, comments, and photos – the things that are scattered – and turns them into a book. Turn them into a punchy graphic novel, or maybe a leisurely hardback volume; whatever best captures those feelings that matter so much.

Invite family and friends into the process and hold onto every detail. Or make it your own and pass it down to your kids. Whatever your story, print and ship something tangible you can see, share, and save as you retell the story again.

Choose your layout, preview it in the app, and order your story as a bound book – comic, hardback, or softback, your call. Hand it to your family. Frame it. It’s a real storybook.

Addressing Your Concerns

Are you saving photos of my kids?

TBD Wording to settle. Photos you upload are kept in your account so your book can use them; they are never shared, distributed, or used for anything but your own stories.

Are you using my photos to train your AI model?

No. We use TBD models from TBD, which are already trained, coded, and prompted. The version you are using is not improved through your use. Neither Campaignia nor our AI providers ever train AI models on your stories or art.

How long does it take to print and ship my story?

Once you are happy with your story and submit it for printing, the file is sent to our printer in TBD. Within 5–6 business days it will be fully printed, packaged, and shipped to your door.

What do I need to paste in?

Anything goes – your notes, a voice-to-text recording, emails, a chat thread, or what you typed up afterwards. The more detail, the richer the output.

How does the AI know which moments matter?

Campaignia finds the turning points, the surprises and the emotional beats automatically. You can also give it director’s notes – tell it exactly which scenes you want included.

Can I use Campaignia for any kind of story?

Absolutely. A holiday, a birthday, a school year, a road trip, the bedtime saga you have been telling for years – anything with a story worth telling.

What does the exported PDF look like?

A print-ready storybook with a cover page, AI-illustrated panels, narrative prose, and your character cast. Four layout styles, and you preview before you print.

Can I use my own photos, or my own writing?

Yes. Family photos, children’s drawings or your own art can go right into the story, and a story you’ve already written stays word for word while Campaignia adds the pictures.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, always. Cancel anytime and keep access through your billing period. Your stories and chapters are always accessible – they don’t disappear.
