FAQs
What do I need to paste in?
Anything goes — your notes, a voice-to-text recording, emails, a chat thread, or what you typed up afterwards. The more detail, the richer the output.
How does the AI know which moments matter?
Campaignia finds the turning points, the surprises and the emotional beats automatically. You can also give it director's notes — tell it exactly which scenes you want included. Click here to see some of the moments other users have made.
Can I use Campaignia for any kind of story?
Absolutely. A holiday, a birthday, a school year, a road trip, the bedtime saga you have been telling for years, or a night around the table — anything with a story worth telling. Campaignia works from what you wrote down, not from a format.
Do I need my own AI API keys?
No. Campaignia handles all AI costs on our end. Your subscription covers everything — no setup required beyond creating your account.
What does the exported PDF look like?
A print-ready storybook with cover page, AI-illustrated panels, narrative prose, and your character cast. Four layout styles, preview before you print. Click here to see our Library.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes, always. Cancel anytime and keep access through your billing period. Your stories and chapters are always accessible — they don't disappear.
