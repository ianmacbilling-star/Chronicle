How it Works

1

Characters

Add reference pictures of your characters and describe their personalities, quirks and look. Already have a character sheet? Upload it, and Campaignia reads all the key details from it. Then it keeps everyone visually consistent across every session.

2

Type, Paste or Upload

Drop in your notes, an outline or the session transcript, or upload the audio recording of the session. Campaignia builds your storyboard around the key moments, turning the chaos at the table into a structured story.

3

Rewrite

Any deviation feels like an insult to your imagination. Put in your own writing or artwork, or use Campaignia’s writing coach to develop your skills.

4

Customize

Generate anything that’s beyond your ability. Edit whole sections or minute details with the click of a button. Campaignia is every tool your book will ever need, in one place.

5

Print, Order, Ship

Choose your layout, preview it in the app, and order your story as a bound book or comic, hardback or softback. Your call. Gift it to your party, or read it to your kids.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Campaignia for any TTRPG system?

Absolutely. D&D, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu, Blades in the Dark, Mothership – any system with a story worth telling. Campaignia works from the transcript, not the ruleset.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, always. Cancel anytime and keep access through your billing period. Your campaigns and sessions are always accessible – your stories don’t disappear.

Is my work safe with AI?

The AI is built into Campaignia, so there are no API keys and nothing to set up. Neither Campaignia nor our AI providers ever train AI models on your stories or art.

What transcript format does Campaignia need?

Anything goes – session notes, Discord logs, Roll20 exports, voice-to-text recordings, or your own summary. The more detail, the richer the output.

Do I have to learn a bunch of prompting strategies?

No. Campaignia needs your transcript and a little direction, and it is built around TTRPGs to keep prompting to a minimum. No advanced or complicated prompting needed.

How does the AI know which moments matter?

Campaignia picks out combat, revelations and emotional beats automatically. You can also give it director’s notes – tell it exactly which scenes you want included. See some of the moments other tables have made.

Can I use my own art, or my own writing?

Yes. Campaignia can do it all, but it doesn’t have to. Illustrate it yourself with your table’s own art, or keep a story you’ve already written word for word and let Campaignia add the pictures. Mix and match as you like.

What does the exported PDF look like?

A print-ready graphic novel with a cover page, illustrated panels, narrative prose and your character cast, in several layout styles you preview before you print. See our Library.

How long does it take to print and ship my book?

About two weeks from the day you order: about a week to print your book and about a week to ship it to your door.

How much does a printed book cost?

Roughly 50 cents a page, plus shipping. You see the exact price before you pay.

Doesn’t AI just threaten artists, and human creativity in general?

In many ways it can. We are musicians, writers and coders ourselves, and we are not blind to the harm AI has done to many creative people and industries. That is why Campaignia includes training tools that encourage creative skill, lets you put in as much of your own art and writing as you like, TBD offers partnerships with artists who take character commissions, and is for strictly personal use.
